About percent — percent bounds are valid, but the fluid interpolation always runs on vw internally, since that's how the linear-slope formula works. Percent behaves as expected on properties like width or padding (relative to the parent element). For font-size, percent isn't a meaningful standalone unit — prefer px / rem / em / vw there.
