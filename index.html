Percent bounds are valid, but the fluid interpolation always runs on vw internally — that's how the linear-slope math works. Percent behaves as expected on properties like width or padding (relative to the parent element). For font-size, percent isn't a meaningful unit on its own — prefer px / rem / em / vw there.
